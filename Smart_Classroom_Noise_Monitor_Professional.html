🤖 How the AI Project Works

1. Listen
The microphone measures relative sound intensity.
2. Classify
The app classifies the room as Quiet, Getting Loud, or Too Loud.
3. Analyze
Noise readings are compared over time to find patterns.
4. Suggest
The dashboard gives a simple classroom-management suggestion.
